
💡 New Changes for 2026

This year, we are doing things a little differently...

It's no secret that AI has changed how Capture the Flag is played. This event is my attempt to keep the spirit of CTF alive, while embracing and encouraging the use of AI.

Compounding challenges introduce three different play modes, each presented as its own scored submission in CourseStack:

"Single Solve" tasks are exactly what you are already used to; solve one static challenge, taking however long you need to, and submit uncovered flag for points.

"Time Trial" tasks ask you to solve one dynamic variant of the same challenge under a time limit. You may need a deterministic solution to beat the clock and get the flag!

"Mastery Mode" tasks ask you to solve multiple dynamic variants of the same challenge, but sometimes even additional challenge concepts compounding from prior day's challenges.

Standard challenges remain as a single answer submission, just as you're used to with traditional CTF events.

Dynamic per-team flag values have been introduced in an effort to prevent potential flag sharing and cheating. Please ensure you read and accept the rules of the game.

What stayed the same
