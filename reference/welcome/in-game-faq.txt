
❓ In-Game FAQ

Here are some more frequently asked questions and answers pertinent to the CTF.

🌐 Do the challenge VMs have Internet access?

Yes, unless stated otherwise in the challenge prompt and description, challenge VMs can reach the Internet.

🔐 How do I use the CourseStack VPN?
In CourseStack, open the event's VPN download action.
Save the generated .ovpn profile for your current playthrough.
Import it into an OpenVPN-compatible client on your own analysis VM.
Connect before using a private target address, then disconnect when you finish.

Treat the profile as private account material. Do not post it in Discord or commit it to a repository. Homebase already receives its own CourseStack network route; do not replace that route or add identity headers.

If the VPN connects but a target does not respond, first check the challenge's current status and connection details. A stale address from an earlier attempt is not reusable proof.

⏰ When are new challenges going to be released?

At 09:00 AM Eastern Time each day, at least one new challenge will be released.

👥 How many players can be on a team?

There is no maximum amount of players that can be on a team. We encourage collaboration within your team, and there is no realistic way to enforce a team-size restriction in a global online game.

👤 Can I participate solo?

Yes! You may be on a team of one and still tackle the challenges at your own pace and leisure.

🏅 Will there be badges or certificates for players?

Yes, at the end of the month we expect to provide participation recognition.
