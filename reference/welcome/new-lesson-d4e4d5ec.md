# 💬 Join Us In Discord

You can join our Discord server to chat with other CTF players and the event staff via the **Help** or **Community** buttons on the left-side navigation bar.

The Huntress CTF uses Discord _Roles_ to allow entry into Discord and enable messaging permissions.

To chat within Discord, you must connect your CTF account with your Discord account using our platform integration. 

1. Go to **[https://ctf.huntress.com](https://ctf.huntress.com)** in your web browser's address bar.
2. Click **Sign In** in the top-right of the page if you are not logged in.
3. Click your account name in the top-right of the page once you are logged in.
4. Click **Settings** from the drop-down menu.

   ![Open Settings from your CTF account menu.](https://images.coursestack.com/a8be0848-ea22-4644-8dda-99af6ac82b7e/bb408ce8-4a50-4b74-8393-0bc776e4994c)

5. Click the **Connect to Discord** button in the Discord section.

   ![Select Connect to Discord from your CTF account settings.](https://images.coursestack.com/a8be0848-ea22-4644-8dda-99af6ac82b7e/e5bcde50-1a0e-4bc8-b943-47eed4f44385)

6. Click **Authorize** on the pop-up presented within Discord.

   ![Authorize CourseStack Bot to connect your Discord account.](https://images.coursestack.com/a8be0848-ea22-4644-8dda-99af6ac82b7e/400b0d63-f658-4812-b78c-1f553d51da72)

7. Click **Close** on the pop-up presented within Discord.

   ![Close the Discord confirmation and return to your browser.](https://images.coursestack.com/a8be0848-ea22-4644-8dda-99af6ac82b7e/c2a788fc-291d-48a7-9e77-45727c7b220d)

8. Observe the CTF platform now sees your Discord account connected!

   ![Confirm that your Discord account is connected in CourseStack.](https://images.coursestack.com/a8be0848-ea22-4644-8dda-99af6ac82b7e/03855b2f-d240-4be7-a19b-c4821e3ffc7d)

Within the Huntress CTF Discord server, your Discord account should now have the role **2026 CTF Participant**.

> [!IMPORTANT] 
> The **2026 CTF Participant** role is what permits you to chat within the channels and open support tickets. 

If you do not see the **2026 CTF Participant** Discord role on your Discord account within the Huntress CTF Discord server, **please click the <u>Sync Roles</u> button under the _Discord_ section of your platform account settings page.**

We hope you join us within the Discord community for memes, GIFs, and CTF shenanigans!
